# Privacy Policy: Calm Grid

_Last updated: [DATE]_

This policy describes how **Calm Grid** ("the app"), published by **[YOUR NAME
OR STUDIO]**, handles information.

## What the app stores on your device

* Your best score, level, coins, unlocked themes, daily streak, daily goals,
  settings and your current unfinished game.
* How often you have accepted or declined "watch an ad" offers, and the
  settings of a small model that learns how to present those offers to you.

This information stays **only on your device**. We don't send it to our own
servers, because we don't run any. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Advertising

The app shows ads through **Google AdMob**: optional rewarded ads that you
choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between games. To serve and
measure ads, Google may collect:

* your device's advertising ID,
* approximate location from your IP address,
* device and app information, and ad interactions.

Google's use of this data is described at
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

If you're in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you'll be asked for consent before
personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the
privacy button in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in
your device settings.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
data from them.
